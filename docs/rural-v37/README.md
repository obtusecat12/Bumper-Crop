# V37 — scattered buildings and farms within the fields

V36 incorrectly replaced the single-building population with fully equipped farms, each surrounded by a large rectangular clearance. V37 restores the earlier eight building forms as the main population through the road-based planner, with fewer two-building groups, occasional farms and rare junction hamlets. Placement still checks real road geometry, existing landmarks, lake margins and the protected arrival area. Old per-tile scatter remains suppressed so two independent placement systems cannot collide.

Seeded road stations vary in spacing, setback, side and occupancy. Adjacent road runs have different occupancy probabilities. A local priority exclusion keeps sites apart without snapping them into a settlement grid. A single site ordinarily contains exactly one original building. Pairs have eight composition families; farms twelve; hamlets four. Width/depth, facing, later additions, finishes and partial boundaries vary independently. The families include tandem, linear, parallel, oblique, staggered, attached wing, open L, dispersed crop islands and an expanded older holding. Some larger holdings retain 15×25m-scale barns. Windbreaks, fences, silos and windpumps are occasional features rather than a compulsory inventory.

Ground clearance is now the union of small door/work ellipses, narrow worn links and building footprints. It does not fill the compound bounding rectangle. Footpaths avoid other structures, leaving crops between detached sheds. Patch edges have restrained continuous variation. Terrain rendering, crop exclusion and map rasterization consume this same sampler, with complete neighboring-chunk context. The authored arrival farm also uses localized pads; its buildings and the fixed Kephart connector remain in place.

The original detailed building generator now handles all eight variants at every LOD. Opt-in compound LODs reduce plank subdivisions and subpixel details while preserving original silhouettes, openings, roof forms, collisions and pickup positions. Six finite timber/masonry finish palettes and separate roof finishes are shared. The existing working-yard generator is restored around these buildings, including tools, sacks, hay, crates and vessels, with stable per-object seeds and road, crop, entrance and neighboring-component rejection. Original standalone/authored geometry remains unchanged.

Troughs now have a real stained bottom and inner walls visible through a transparent water plane. Two existing mipmapped water-normal textures animate subtle ripples; Fresnel reflection increases at grazing angles. A small edge meniscus and stained historical waterline establish the depth. No scene refraction target is added. Shared normal textures, materials and time bindings survive worker transfer and chunk disposal; water surfaces do not cast solid shadows.

## Validation scope

- Grammar audit: 48,000 seeded layouts, zero reserved-footprint overlaps; 2,000 work-patch layouts, zero links crossing reserved structures.
- Model audit: all eight original standalone variants preserve exact geometry, collisions, pickups and roof records. Compound LODs preserve physical/interaction metadata and yard composition. Shared packet resources are retained and reused.
- Native water inspection uses actual shader programs, surface textures and trough geometry. Images are diagnostic renders, not screenshots of full gameplay.
- Actual-world audit over 1.92 km²: 17 singles, 6 pairs and 1 farm, versus 43 all-farm sites in V36; all eight old variants appear. Of 44,928 m² inside site bounds, 31,092 m² remains natural; only 2,488 m² is working-patch dirt.
- 5,025 samples in the protected 20 m disk retain exact height, road, crop, wheat and vegetation state. 3,342 boundary samples show no seams; 640 water/shore samples retain unchanged lake descriptors and heights. All 32 sampled building footprints have safe map landings. 95,318 generated cereal roots have no footprint, work-patch or strong-rut intrusions.
- Native views show an actual single red barn with restored sacks/crates, an actual two-shed pair, and an actual dispersed farm surrounded by cereals. These are source-geometry diagnostic renders, not production gameplay screenshots.
- Full first-person browser gameplay and frame rate are not verified: the managed browser cannot create WebGL2. Browser inspection covers the actual map generator; native GLES covers selected actual scene meshes and materials with diagnostic lighting.

The structural direction is consistent with the loose, dispersed, linear and route-based layouts distinguished by Historic England's *Historic Farmsteads: A Manual for Recording* (2017): https://historicengland.org.uk/images-books/publications/historic-farmsteads-manual-for-recording/historic-farmsteads-manual-recording/ . The scene's probabilities and mix are art direction, not historical statistics.
