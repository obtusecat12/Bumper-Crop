# Lakeshore outpost refinement

The existing outpost retains its world center and shoreline intake. Its perimeter is now 44 × 42 m (formerly 60 × 54 m), with varied tent orientations and coherent rotated furniture, collision, lamps and wiring. The kitchen and two dormitories have a single entrance. All four taps begin closed, and the reflective wet area is limited to the trough vicinity. Two furnished cots are added inside the container.

Four new images were generated: aged mahogany, a cooker detail atlas, transparent wheat sheaves, and a black MEG eagle/cube emblem using the supplied reference. Prompts and original PNGs are retained in this directory. Runtime output is five WebP files, totaling about 350 KiB; the cooker atlas is cropped into top and front surfaces. Hay uses alpha-tested crossed cards with no solid hay core. The enlarged double-pedestal desk faces the office entrance.

Local browser detail views and full-game inside/water views are in `tests/lake-outpost/results`. Full-game screenshots use the actual renderer after terrain streaming, with no browser or shader errors. These are SwiftShader visual checks, not hardware performance measurements.
