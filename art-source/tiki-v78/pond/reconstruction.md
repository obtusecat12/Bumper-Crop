## Hidden regions and implementation limits

The rear of the statue, submerged basin floor/plumbing, exact emitter housings, thickness of rear wall, left rear room plan, and off-frame bar length are unobserved. Treat these as low-confidence completion geometry and avoid extra props. No real-world scale is supplied; use the ratio estimates above to match silhouette before deciding dimensions. The two photos may have been taken at different times, so water ripples/foam, flowers, and movable tabletop props should not be forced into an exact shared moment.

## Material delivery

`moai-resin-atlas.png` is 1536×1024, 3 columns by 2 rows, six 512×512 exact crops. Top row: basecolor, OpenGL +Y normal, roughness. Bottom row: AO, height, metalness. The generator's black metallic cell includes residual values 0–28; set numeric material metalness to 0 to guarantee a nonmetal surface. Normal and displacement must remain shallow so texture does not change statue geometry.

`foam-breakup.png` is 1254×1254 RGBA. Actual alpha spans 0–255, with 44.42% fully transparent pixels and 55.56% partially transparent pixels. Use its native alpha. It provides irregular joined rafts and internal holes with varied fine cells, not a sheet of identical white dot sprites. Overlay on water at low opacity and let pond lights supply yellow/green color.

Existing v77 `pond-masonry` material already matches pale weathered block stone sufficiently and was not regenerated. Use the geometry to establish block courses; avoid adding a conflicting fine block grid from repeating the whole masonry tile too often.
